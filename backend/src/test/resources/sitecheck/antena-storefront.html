Lexis Inn Beach Resort – sea-facing rooms in Diveagar

Lexis Inn Beach Resort is a family-run beach hotel two minutes from Diveagar beach on the Konkan coast of Maharashtra. We have twelve AC rooms, a garden, and a kitchen that serves home-style Konkan fish thali, modak and breakfast made each morning. Guests come for the quiet beach, the Suvarna Ganesh temple, dolphin boat rides from Harihareshwar and the weekend drive from Pune and Mumbai.

Direct bookings on this website are confirmed instantly and carry no OTA commission, so the price you see is the price you pay. We accept UPI, cards and net banking, and send the booking confirmation on WhatsApp and email. Check-in is at 12 noon and check-out at 10 am; early check-in is given when the room is ready.

The resort is 170 km from Pune and 190 km from Mumbai by road. Shared taxis run from Mangaon railway station, which is on the Konkan line, and MSRTC buses stop at Diveagar bus stand five minutes from the gate. Parking is free for guests and the road to the beach is lit at night.

Nearby you can visit Harihareshwar temple, Shrivardhan beach, Bankot fort and the Velas turtle festival in March. We arrange boat rides, bicycle hire and a bonfire on the lawn on request. Pets are welcome in the garden rooms. The kitchen caters for vegetarian and Jain guests when told in advance.

Rooms come with hot water, a ceiling fan and air conditioning, fresh linen, towels and filtered drinking water. Wi-Fi covers the rooms and the dining hall. A doctor is on call in Diveagar village and the nearest hospital is in Shrivardhan, twenty minutes away. Long-stay discounts apply for stays of five nights or more during the week.
