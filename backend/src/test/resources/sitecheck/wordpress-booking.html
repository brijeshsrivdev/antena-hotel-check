Betel Nut Resort

Six cottages in a working betel nut plantation, five minutes on foot from Diveagar beach. Home-cooked Konkan food, hammocks, a plunge pool and silence after nine. The resort is run by the Patil family, who have farmed this land for four generations and opened the cottages to guests in 2015.

Each cottage sleeps two adults and two children and has a private verandah, attic bed, hot water and a fan; four of the six are air conditioned. Breakfast is included and dinner is served in the plantation under the trees. We are 180 km from Pune and 200 km from Mumbai, with the Konkan railway station at Mangaon forty minutes away.

Things to do: the beach at sunrise, the Suvarna Ganesh temple in the village, Harihareshwar and Shrivardhan beaches, Bankot fort and the Velas turtle festival. We lend bicycles and arrange boat trips with the local fishermen. Pets are welcome in two of the cottages.
